# 16 & 17. Row and Column Exchanges

Rearranging rows or columns of a matrix can be done directly by matrix multiplication using a **permutation matrix** $P$:

* **Multiply on the LEFT ($PA$):** Swaps **ROWS**
* **Multiply on the RIGHT ($AP$):** Swaps **COLUMNS**

> **Memory Rule:**
> * **LEFT $\longrightarrow$ ROWS** ($PA$)
> * **RIGHT $\longrightarrow$ COLUMNS** ($AP$)

---

## 1. Column Exchange: Multiplying on the RIGHT ($AP$)

Suppose:

$$
A =
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 2 \\
3 & 4
\end{bmatrix}
$$

The two columns are:

$$
C_1 = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 3 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad C_2 = \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 4 \end{bmatrix}
$$

To swap Column 1 and Column 2 ($C_1 \leftrightarrow C_2$), multiply $A$ on the **right** by:

$$
P =
\begin{bmatrix}
0 & 1 \\
1 & 0
\end{bmatrix}
$$

### Middle Calculations for $AP$:

$$
AP =
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 2 \\
3 & 4
\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}
0 & 1 \\
1 & 0
\end{bmatrix}
$$

Using **Row $\times$ Column**:
* **Entry $(1, 1)$:** $1(0) + 2(1) = 0 + 2 = 2$
* **Entry $(1, 2)$:** $1(1) + 2(0) = 1 + 0 = 1$
* **Entry $(2, 1)$:** $3(0) + 4(1) = 0 + 4 = 4$
* **Entry $(2, 2)$:** $3(1) + 4(0) = 3 + 0 = 3$

$$
\boxed{AP =
\begin{bmatrix}
2 & 1 \\
4 & 3
\end{bmatrix}}
$$

Notice: The two columns swapped places!

---

## 2. Row Exchange: Multiplying on the LEFT ($PA$)

Now let's take the same matrix $A$ and swap its **rows** ($R_1 \leftrightarrow R_2$).

Multiply on the **left** by $P$:

$$
PA =
\begin{bmatrix}
0 & 1 \\
1 & 0
\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 2 \\
3 & 4
\end{bmatrix}
$$

### Middle Calculations for $PA$:

Using **Row $\times$ Column**:
* **Entry $(1, 1)$:** $0(1) + 1(3) = 0 + 3 = 3$
* **Entry $(1, 2)$:** $0(2) + 1(4) = 0 + 4 = 4$
* **Entry $(2, 1)$:** $1(1) + 0(3) = 1 + 0 = 1$
* **Entry $(2, 2)$:** $1(2) + 0(4) = 2 + 0 = 2$

$$
\boxed{PA =
\begin{bmatrix}
3 & 4 \\
1 & 2
\end{bmatrix}}
$$

Notice: The two rows swapped places!

---

## 3. Comparison Summary ($2 \times 2$)

Starting Matrix:

$$
A =
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 2 \\
3 & 4
\end{bmatrix}
$$

* **Row swap ($PA$ on LEFT):**
  $$
  PA =
  \begin{bmatrix}
  0 & 1 \\
  1 & 0
  \end{bmatrix}
  \begin{bmatrix}
  1 & 2 \\
  3 & 4
  \end{bmatrix}
  =
  \begin{bmatrix}
  3 & 4 \\
  1 & 2
  \end{bmatrix}
  $$

* **Column swap ($AP$ on RIGHT):**
  $$
  AP =
  \begin{bmatrix}
  1 & 2 \\
  3 & 4
  \end{bmatrix}
  \begin{bmatrix}
  0 & 1 \\
  1 & 0
  \end{bmatrix}
  =
  \begin{bmatrix}
  2 & 1 \\
  4 & 3
  \end{bmatrix}
  $$

## 4. Example with 3 Columns ($2 \times 3$)

Suppose:

$$
A =
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 2 & 3 \\
4 & 5 & 6
\end{bmatrix}
$$

We want to swap **Column 1 and Column 3** ($C_1 \leftrightarrow C_3$).

Take the $3 \times 3$ identity matrix and swap its columns 1 and 3 to build $P$:

$$
P =
\begin{bmatrix}
0 & 0 & 1 \\
0 & 1 & 0 \\
1 & 0 & 0
\end{bmatrix}
$$

Multiply on the **right** ($AP$):

$$
AP =
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 2 & 3 \\
4 & 5 & 6
\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}
0 & 0 & 1 \\
0 & 1 & 0 \\
1 & 0 & 0
\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Middle calculations:**
* **Row 1:**
  * Col 1: $1(0) + 2(0) + 3(1) = 3$
  * Col 2: $1(0) + 2(1) + 3(0) = 2$
  * Col 3: $1(1) + 2(0) + 3(0) = 1$
* **Row 2:**
  * Col 1: $4(0) + 5(0) + 6(1) = 6$
  * Col 2: $4(0) + 5(1) + 6(0) = 5$
  * Col 3: $4(1) + 5(0) + 6(0) = 4$

$$
\boxed{AP =
\begin{bmatrix}
3 & 2 & 1 \\
6 & 5 & 4
\end{bmatrix}}
$$

Columns 1 and 3 have successfully swapped!

## ⭐ Quick Cheat Sheet

| Operation | Multiplication | Formula |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Swap Rows** | Multiply on the **LEFT** | $\mathbf{PA}$ |
| **Swap Columns** | Multiply on the **RIGHT** | $\mathbf{AP}$ |
